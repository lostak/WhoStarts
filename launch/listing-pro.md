# Store listing: WhoStarts Pro (paid)

Package: `com.lostak.whostarts.pro`
Play Console path: **Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing**

Use this listing only once the free app shows banner ads. Until then the two builds are the same app, and Play can treat a paid copy of a free app as repetitive content. See "Release blockers" in `launch/README.md`.

## Pricing

Play Console path: **Monetize with Play > Products > App pricing**

- Set the app to **Paid** before the very first release on any track. Play never lets a free app become paid later.
- A price needs a payments profile (merchant account) linked to your developer account first.
- Suggested price: **$1.99 USD**, then let Play convert it for other countries. Low enough to be an impulse buy for someone tired of the banner.
