# WhoStarts Privacy Policy

Covers WhoStarts and WhoStarts Pro for Android. Last updated: November 17, 2026

This is the same policy published at https://lostak.github.io/WhoStarts/privacy.html (source: `docs/privacy.html`). Keep the two in sync.

**In short:** everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't have permission to use the internet.

## Who makes the app

WhoStarts and WhoStarts Pro are pool game trackers made by Bradley Lostak. This policy explains what the apps do with your information.

## What the app stores

The app saves the team names, player names, team colors, solids or stripes picks, and game results you enter, along with the time each game was recorded. This is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not request internet access.

## Device backups

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's saved data in your device backup to your own Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings. We never receive it and cannot see it.

## Ads

WhoStarts Pro shows no ads. The free WhoStarts app does not currently show ads. If ads are added to the free app, this policy will be updated before that version is released to say which ad provider is used and what it collects.

## Links

The "Privacy policy" item in the app's menu opens this policy in your web browser. Your browser's own privacy settings apply there.

## Deleting your data

You can delete matchups and their results inside the app at any time from each matchup's settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it saved on your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
