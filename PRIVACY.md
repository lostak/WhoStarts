# WhoStarts Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

WhoStarts is a pool game tracker made by Bradley Lostak. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores

WhoStarts saves the team names, player names, colors, and game results you enter. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app collects

WhoStarts does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

## Deleting your data

You can delete teams and results inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything it saved.

## Children

WhoStarts is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If the app adds features that change what it collects, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
